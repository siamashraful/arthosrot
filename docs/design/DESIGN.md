# DESIGN.md — Arthosrot

> **Purpose:** the as-built record — where the app uses each piece of the Arthosrot Design System, and what the app adds on top of it. Ground truth for anyone styling a surface.
> **Audience:** every design/UI session. **Source of truth for the system itself:** the Claude Design artifact <https://claude.ai/artifact/H15gE6qMYiLEQqbMFdiuXh> ("Arthosrot Updated Design System"). **Rationale lives in** [brand/BRAND.md](brand/BRAND.md); the token tables and class contract in [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md); a11y method in [ACCESSIBILITY.md](ACCESSIBILITY.md); the adoption decision in [../architecture/adr/ADR-013-design-system-adoption.md](../architecture/adr/ADR-013-design-system-adoption.md).

## The world

**Colour-block clarity.** A slightly cool off-white canvas (`--bg`) carrying white cards (`--surface`, 20px radius, no border, no shadow) and **one near-black hero card per screen** (`--hero`, 24px radius); saturated bloom colours as category containers and icon chips; lime for selection and focus only; green and red exclusively for gain and loss. Pill buttons, hairline separation, no gradients, no glass. Dark theme: `canvas-dark` → `surface-dark` → `surface-raised` as surface steps; the hero sits on `surface-dark` like every other card.

Register: **Product UI, Operate** — design serves the task. Numbers are the interface.

## Type

- **Figtree** 400 / 600 / 700, self-hosted, `font-display: swap`. Body = `var(--font-sans)`, `tabular-nums` on `body`.
- The scale is the system's: `.ar-display` … `.ar-micro` (or the `--display-*` … `--micro-*` tokens). `h1` = title, `h2`/`h3` = heading.
- Hero numeral: `.ar-hero__value` (alias `.hero-value`) at display 40/44/700 — the boldest thing on screen.
- Bengali renders **only** as outlined vector inside the brand SVGs — never live `<text>`.
- All financial formatting via `lib/format.ts`; components never hand-format numbers.

## Colour

- Role tokens only in components (`--bg`, `--surface`, `--raised`, `--text`, `--text-secondary`, `--text-tertiary`, `--divider`, `--hero`, `--on-hero`, `--primary`, `--on-primary` …). Raw hex, spacing or z literals in a component are review-blocking.
- **Semantic text rule:** `.gain` / `.loss` (and `.ar-delta--gain/--loss`, `.ar-row__value--gain/--loss`) use `--gain-text` / `--loss-text`; the full `--gain` / `--loss` hue is for arrows, icons, chart fills and numerals ≥ 24px. On the hero: `--hero-gain` / `--hero-loss`.
- **Slate deviation:** the system's `slate` (#6b7280) reads **4.43:1 on the canvas** although its usage note allows captions there. The app sets `--text-tertiary` (light) one step deeper, **#666d7a** (4.77:1 on the canvas, 5.21:1 on paper), so captions may sit on either ground; the contrast gate holds 4.5:1 on both. Flagged upstream — revert to the system's value when it corrects it.
- **White on mustard / blush / tangerine is not used in the app.** The system permits white on all five saturated blooms as a stated brand choice (mustard 1.55:1, blush 3.05:1, tangerine 3.03:1); the axe scan in e2e would fail on mustard, so Cash renders on the paper-and-tint tile instead (below), and the gate checks only `on-bloom` on `cobalt` and `violet`.
- BUY and SELL are neutral: side is a labelled segmented control, the confirm is the primary pill either way. `.ar-btn--destructive` (loss fill) exists only at a final sell/close or cancel confirmation.

### App-shell extensions (the app's own, in the same grammar)

The system is phone-first; these are the product's additions, built from its tokens and idioms:

- **Desktop sidebar** `.shell-nav` / `.nav-link` — the tab bar stood upright: surface column, hairline right edge, 44px rows, lime leading-edge indicator on `aria-current="page"`.
- **Tables** `.data-table` (`.collapsible`) — kept on wide screens for orders and holdings (sortable, comparable columns): hairline separation, caption-weight headers in `--text-tertiary`, tabular numerals right-aligned; ≤767 each row becomes a two-line list item.
- **Native `<dialog>`** instead of the bundle's static sheet markup, so focus trapping, Esc and `::backdrop` stay native.
- **The mode ribbon** and **`[data-mode="live"]` overlays** — the paper/live system (BRAND.md §5), which the design system does not define.
- **Explainer** `.explainer` — in-place `<details>` teaching, caption size, lime dot when open.
- **Onboarding slider** — a range input on the hero: `--hero-pill` track, `--on-hero` fill, lime thumb with an ink ring (the system's scrubber handle).

## Iconography

**Phosphor, duotone** (ADR-014 — chosen by the owner over the system's generic Lucide-style set), through `src/components/icons/Icon.tsx` — `<Icon name="…" size={…} />` with semantic names, `aria-hidden` by default. Duotone for pictorial glyphs, regular for controls, bold for ≤ 16px typographic glyphs, fill for an on-state. **Never another library, never a Phosphor import outside `Icon.tsx`.** Weights and the size scale (24 default · 22 in 40px chips · 20 tile heads and row chevrons · 18 in `--sm` chips and hero pills · 14 at stroke 2 in `--xs` chips · delta arrows 14 at stroke 2.25) are in DESIGN_SYSTEM.md → Iconography.

**Chips follow the system's Home, Activity and Invest screens** — a category chip names an asset class, a semantic chip names a money movement or a state, and nothing is tinted for decoration:

**Glyph-only places:** sidebar and tab bar — Dashboard `home`, Portfolio `pie`, **Search** `search` (the `/markets` destination is labelled by what you do there: find a stock; browse sits below the field), Orders `orders`, Activity `activity`, Settings `settings` (24px, no container); the alerts bell `bell` (24px; top bar and sidebar, ADR-016); hero pills — Trade `trade`, Portfolio `pie`, Activity `activity` (18px); the Stocks BloomTile head — `stocks` (20px); theme control `sun` / `moon` / `monitor`; sign out `logout`.

**Withdrawn:** the unboxed `MetricIcon` beside metric labels and the "a deposit is the Cash chip" rule. The system's semantic chips win: insight cards carry one tinted chip, and money in is a gain chip.

## Motion policy

CSS transitions only: `--duration-fast` 150ms for pressed states, chip selection and toggles; `--duration-base` 250ms for sheet open, tab change and the fill bar; always `--ease-out-expo`, always from an already visible state. **No entrance animation on data** — nothing staggers in, nothing moves when a value refreshes. `pulse-in` on `OrderStatusBadge` state change and `sheet-up` on dialog open are the only keyframes. No celebratory motion on fills, gains, losses or the paper→live switch. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → near-instant, single iteration. The `motion` package was removed (ADR-013 supersedes ADR-012).

## Verification contract

Any styling change must keep green:

- `pnpm check:contrast` — **78 pairs** computed from the system's own usage notes over `src/styles/tokens.css`: 35 per theme (text roles on bg/surface/raised, on-primary, the six semantic `-text` on surface and on tint, the full gain/loss hues at 3:1, lime-text, `on-tint` on the five tints, each category hue on its tint, `on-bloom` on cobalt and violet, and the four hero pairs) plus the four hero pairs re-run on both live-mode composites. **Never weaken pairs — extend them.**
- The axe scans inside `pnpm test:e2e` (serious/critical fail the build).
- Both themes at **390px and 1360px**, with `[data-mode="live"]` checked in both.

The texture grain, the black live hero, the ribbon label, the open corner of the mark and the bilingual lockup spacing are identity and safety — not available for "cleanup".
